# Your Complete Plan — Cycle, Training, Meals & Recovery

Built for: **31 y / 6'0" / 170 lb**, Mon–Fri **5:30 AM** training, Sat–Sun rest,
rice + chicken/steak diet, whey + creatine, Steel Ultimate Mass Stack (andro) →
Alpha-AF PCT.

> ⚕️ **Before day one:** get baseline blood work (lipids, liver enzymes,
> total/free testosterone) and clear the plan with a physician. Repeat the
> panel after PCT. Zero alcohol from the first dose to the last PCT capsule.
> This document organizes *your* stated protocol — it is not medical advice.

## 1. The Cycle Calendar

Two calendars below — the app also supports a 6-week middle path (pick it in
Settings; the phases scale the same way: 42 active days → 30-day PCT →
8-week recovery). It defaults to the conservative 4-week run, which is the
right call for a first run of this stack.

### Option A — 4-week cycle (recommended first run)

| Phase | Dates | What you take |
|---|---|---|
| **Active cycle** (4 wk) | **Mon Jul 6 → Sun Aug 2, 2026** | 1 tablet of each andro product, 2× daily, 10–12 h apart, empty stomach — every day incl. weekends |
| **PCT** (30 days) | **Mon Aug 3 → Tue Sep 1, 2026** | Alpha-AF: 3 capsules with your first meal, every day. No andro products. |
| **Recovery** (8 wk) | **Wed Sep 2 → Tue Oct 27, 2026** | Nothing hormonal. Whey + creatine continue. Train and eat like it's still on-cycle. |
| Next cycle eligible | **Wed Oct 28, 2026** | Only if blood work is back to baseline |

### Option B — 8-week cycle (experienced users only, monitor tolerance)

| Phase | Dates |
|---|---|
| Active cycle (8 wk) | Mon Jul 6 → Sun Aug 30, 2026 |
| PCT (30 days) | Mon Aug 31 → Tue Sep 29, 2026 |
| Recovery (8 wk) | Wed Sep 30 → Tue Nov 24, 2026 |

**Hard rules for the whole run**
- Doses 10–12 h apart on an **empty stomach**; wait 30–60 min before any calories.
- PM dose ideally ≥2 h after your last meal.
- Never skip PCT, never extend the cycle "because it's going well."
- Calorie surplus every single day — never cut on cycle.

## 2. The Daily Schedule (Mon–Fri, training days)

| Time | What | Why it's timed this way |

| **5:30–6:30 AM** | **Train** (split below) | 60 min after dose — absorption window is closed |

| 10:30 AM | **Meal 2:** 6 oz chicken + 1.5 cups rice | |

| Time | What |

| 9:00 AM | 20–40 min easy walk (active recovery) |

**During PCT:** delete the two andro dose rows; add **Alpha-AF 3 caps at
Meal 1** (7:15 AM weekdays / 9:15 AM weekends). Everything else — training,
meals, creatine, sleep — stays identical for all 30 days.

## 3. Nutrition Targets (170 lb → growing)

| Target | Number | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Calories | **~3,100–3,300 kcal/day** | TDEE ≈ 2,750 + surplus. Never below maintenance on cycle. |
| Protein | **~200–240 g** (≥1 g/lb) | Meat + eggs + 2–3 scoops whey gets you there |
| Carbs | **~400–430 g** | Rice does the heavy lifting, front-loaded around training |
| Fat | **~70–80 g** | Eggs, steak, added olive oil/butter |
| Water | **1 gallon** | Plus deliberate sodium, potassium, magnesium |
| Creatine | **5 g every day** | In the post-workout shake; any time on rest days |

**The feedback loop:** weigh yourself every **Friday at 4:30 AM** (before
food, after bathroom) and log it in the app.
- Gaining **< 0.5 lb/week** → add ½ cup rice to meals 2 and 3.
- Gaining **> 1 lb/week** → drop ½ cup rice from meal 2.

## 4. The Training Split (5:30–6:30 AM)

Progression rule: hit the top of the rep range on **every** set → add 5 lb
(upper body) or 10 lb (lower body) next session. The app flags PRs
automatically and pays bonus XP.

| Day | Session | Core lifts |
|---|---|---|
| **Mon** | Push — Chest & Triceps | Bench 4×6–8 · Incline DB 3×8–10 · Dips 3×8–12 · Cable fly 3×12–15 · OH extension 3×10–12 · Pushdown 3×12–15 |
| **Tue** | Pull — Back & Biceps | Deadlift 3×5 · Pull-up 4×6–10 · BB row 3×8–10 · Face pull 3×15 · BB curl 3×8–12 · Hammer curl 3×10–12 |
| **Wed** | Legs | Squat 4×6–8 · RDL 3×8–10 · Leg press 3×10–12 · Lunge 3×12/leg · Calf raise 4×12–15 · Hanging leg raise 3×12–15 |
| **Thu** | Shoulders & Arms | OHP 4×6–8 · Lateral raise 4×12–15 · Rear delt fly 3×15 · EZ curl 3×8–12 · Skullcrusher 3×8–12 · Shrug 3×12 |
| **Fri** | Full Body — Power & Pump | Front squat 3×6 · Incline bench 3×6–8 · Weighted chin 3×6–8 · Cable row 3×10–12 · Arms superset 3×12+12 · Farmer's carry 3×40 yd |
| **Sat/Sun** | Rest | Walk 20–40 min, eat, sleep. That's the job. |

On-cycle strength climbs fast — **let the reps stay crisp**. No grinding
maximal singles; joints and tendons don't adapt as fast as muscle on
andro compounds.

## 6. Where the App Fits

The **MuscleQuest** Android app (in `muscle-quest/`) turns all of the above
into a daily game: every dose, meal, workout, water goal and bedtime is a
checkbox worth XP; workouts pay 50 XP; PRs pay 30; perfect days pay a bonus
that grows with your streak; achievements pay out up to 300. It knows which
phase you're in from the calendar, swaps andro doses for Alpha-AF
automatically on PCT day 1, and shifts the schedule on weekends. Reminders
are phase-aware too: dose + wake-up (4:30 AM weekdays / 6:30 AM weekends on
cycle, Alpha-AF at breakfast during PCT), the PM dose window (4:30 / 6:30 PM,
on-cycle only), and an evening wind-down — scheduled as exact alarms so Doze
can't hold them past the dose window. Set your cycle start date in Settings
and it builds this entire plan for you, every day, for the full run.
